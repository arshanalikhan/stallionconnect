FAQ

Questions we often hear

Can we start with a small pilot?+

Yes. Most clients start with a small team for a few weeks, review the results, then scale up.

How quickly can a team go live?+

It depends on team size and training needs. A small pilot can usually go live within a few weeks of the process being agreed.

Do you handle international processes?+

Yes. We run domestic and international voice and non-voice processes, with shifts planned around your customers' time zones.

How do you protect our customer data?+

Access is limited to the people working on your process, staff sign confidentiality agreements, and we follow your data-handling rules.

How will we track performance?+

You get regular reports on the KPIs we agree at the start, such as calls handled, conversion, quality scores and turnaround times.
